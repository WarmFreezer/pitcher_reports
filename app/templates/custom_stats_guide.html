Custom Stats Guide

Reference for the custom stats your school's own uploaded report script adds to the pitcher report, on top of the default stats every school gets. These formulas are specific to your school's custom script and can change if it's re-uploaded — this page documents whatever is currently live, not a fixed contract.

  • Overall Stats
  • Pitch Type Contact Stats
  • Pitch Type Execution & Location

Overall Stats

One set of numbers for the whole outing/selection, aggregated across every pitch the pitcher threw.

Impact
Blended score combining swing decision, swinging-strike rate, overall execution, and the inverse of damage — a single-number read on the outing. (Swing Decision + Swinging Strike + Overall Execution + (1 − Damage)) / 4
Swing Decision
Share of the hitter's swing/no-swing decisions that were bad ones: chasing a pitch outside the zone, or taking one in the zone. (swings outside the zone + takes inside the zone) / all swing-or-take decisions
Swinging Strike
Swinging strikes as a share of every pitch thrown — not just swings (compare to Whiff, below, which divides by swings). swinging strikes / all pitches
Whiff
Swing-and-miss rate on swings taken. swinging strikes / (swinging strikes + fouls + balls in play)
Damage
Of all balls in play, the share hit hard on a line — exit velo 90+ mph with a launch angle between 10° and 35°. (exit velo >= 90 mph AND launch angle 10°–35°) / balls in play
Overall Execution
Early and Late Execution blended into one number, weighted by how many pitches went into each — so a pitcher who worked mostly early counts isn't dragged up or down by a handful of late-count pitches. 3-0/3-1 counts aren't part of either, so they're left out here and scored separately below.
Early Execution
In counts of 0-0, 0-1, 1-0, and 1-1, the share of pitches that landed in the strike zone. pitches in the zone / pitches thrown, in those counts
Late Execution
In 0-2 and 1-2, pitches that landed in the zone or in the "around the heart" band — inside the zone padded a foot out, but outside the heart of the plate. In 2-0, 2-1, 2-2, and 3-2, pitches that landed in the zone. Either way, divided by every pitch thrown in those same counts.
3-0/3-1 Execution
In 3-0 and 3-1 counts, the share of pitches called a strike by any means (called, swinging, foul, or in play) — only landing a strike counts here, location doesn't matter, since falling further behind from a 3-ball count is costly regardless of how close the pitch was. strikes (any kind) / pitches thrown, in 3-0 and 3-1 counts
Putaway
Of every pitch thrown with two strikes, the share that ended the at-bat with a strikeout (called or swinging). two-strike pitches resulting in a strikeout / all two-strike pitches
R2K
Share of hitters faced that the pitcher got to a 2-strike count (0-2 or 1-2) before reaching a 2-ball count. Counted once per hitter, not per pitch — a foul-ball battle at an ahead 2-strike count doesn't inflate this. Plate appearances split across a pitching change are excluded entirely; only at-bats this pitcher threw start-to-finish count. hitters reaching 2 strikes before 2 balls / hitters faced
Non-Competitive
Share of pitches that landed more than 3 feet from the center of the zone — pitches nowhere near competitive, regardless of count. pitches > 3 ft from zone center / all pitches
Ground Ball / Line Drive / Air Ball / Popup
Balls in play bucketed by launch angle — Ground ≤ 10°, Line 10°–25°, Air 25°–50°, Pop above 50° — each as a share of all balls in play.

Pitch Type Contact Stats

One row per pitch type thrown. Same definitions as the Overall Stats above, computed within that pitch type only.

Impact
Same blend as the overall Impact score, using this pitch type's own Swing Decision, Sw Str, Overall Execution, and Damage.
Sw Dec
This pitch type's Swing Decision — see above.
Sw Str
This pitch type's Swinging Strike rate, over every pitch of that type.
Whiff
This pitch type's Whiff rate, over swings at that type.
Damage
This pitch type's Damage rate, over balls in play of that type.
Non-Cpv
This pitch type's Non-Competitive rate.
GB / LD / AB / Pop
This pitch type's batted-ball mix, over balls in play of that type.

Pitch Type Execution & Location

One row per pitch type thrown — execution and plate-location mix, broken out by type.

Ov Exec / Early Exec / Late Exec / 3-0/3-1 Exec
This pitch type's Overall, Early, Late, and 3-0/3-1 Execution — same definitions as the Overall Stats section, computed only on pitches of this type.
Inner / Middle / Outer
Share of this pitch type's pitches landing in the inner, middle, or outer third of the strike zone (split by horizontal location, zone height only). pitches in that third of the zone / pitches of this type
