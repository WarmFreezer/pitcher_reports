Safer accounts, password resets, and a few fixes

Here's what's new in Stat_Line.

Email verification

New accounts now confirm their email address before logging in. After you sign up, we send a verification link that's good for 24 hours. If it expires, trying to log in sends a fresh one. Existing accounts don't need to do anything. If you change the email on your account, you'll be asked to verify the new address.

Reset your password

Forgot your password? Use Reset Password on the login page. We email you a 6-digit code, and you enter it with a new password. Codes expire after 15 minutes.

News & updates

Product updates now live on a News page, linked from the footer of every page. This email is the first one. Newsletter emails are optional: you can turn them on or off at any time under Account, and every email has an unsubscribe link.

Fixes

  • The organization sign-up form no longer shows bright white text boxes in dark mode.
  • On the dashboard, the Team Overview title and Download Excel button now line up, with space between them and the table.
  • The Custom Stats Guide no longer shows in the menu for schools without a custom report. Schools that have one find it from the Stats Guide page.

A Custom Stats Guide written for your school

Custom stats differ from school to school, so each school's Custom Stats Guide now documents that school's own stats and formulas instead of a shared one. If your program has custom stats, you'll find your guide from the Stats Guide page.
